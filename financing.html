Questions

Financing FAQ

What do I need to apply?

Usually a copy of your national ID or passport, KRA PIN, 6 months of bank statements and payslips (or business records if self-employed). We'll confirm exactly what your lender needs.

How much deposit do I need?

Most lenders ask for 20–30% of the vehicle price. A larger deposit lowers your monthly payment and total interest.

How long does approval take?

Many applications get a decision within 48 hours once documents are complete.

Can I trade in my current car?

Yes. We value your car on the spot and the agreed amount can go towards your deposit.

Can I finance a foreign-used vehicle?

Yes. Our partners finance both new and foreign-used vehicles, subject to age and valuation limits.
