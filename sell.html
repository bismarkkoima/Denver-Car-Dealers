Selling FAQ

How is my offer worked out?

We look at the make, model, year, mileage, condition and service history, plus current demand in the Kenyan market. Your photos help us give a firm figure faster.

Can I sell a car that still has a loan?

Yes. Tell us in the form and we'll work with your bank or lender to clear the balance as part of the sale.

What documents will I need?

The original logbook, your ID and KRA PIN. If there's a loan, a settlement letter from your lender.

Do I have to buy a car from you?

No. You can sell outright for cash, or use the value as a deposit on any car in our inventory.
