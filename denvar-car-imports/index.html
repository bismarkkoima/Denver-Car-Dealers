The process

From auction bid to your logbook

Four stages, one point of contact. You get a WhatsApp update at every stage — no chasing agents.

01

Source & bid

We shortlist units against your budget and specs, then bid at Japanese or UK auction on your behalf.

02

Inspect & ship

Pre-shipment inspection, then consolidation into a container bound for Mombasa.

03

Clear & duty

KRA duty computed against the current CRSP, KEBS roadworthiness check, and bond release.

04

Register & deliver

NTSA registration and number plates arranged, then delivery to Nairobi or your nearest town.
