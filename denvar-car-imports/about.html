Since 2016

Built by two clearing agents who were tired of watching people get overcharged.

Denvar started as a two-person clearing desk at the Mombasa port, walking client files through KRA by hand. We still clear our own containers — we just also source the cars now, so there's one company accountable for the whole trip, not three.
