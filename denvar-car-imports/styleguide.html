Design system

Style guide

Everything here is rendered from css/tokens.css. Change a token there and this page, and the whole site, follows. Later phases (per-world theming, the multiverse hub) override these same tokens.

Color

A green ramp for surfaces, a gold ramp for emphasis, cream and ink for text.

Typography

Fraunces for headlines and numbers, Inter for body and interface text.

--text-smPrices update weekly with the exchange rate.

Spacing

A 4px-based scale. Use these instead of one-off pixel values.

Liquid glass

Three levels of frost over a colorful backdrop, so the blur is visible.

Base--glass over --glass-strong, 22px blur. Nav, cards, forms.
Strong.glass-strong. For panels that must lift off a busy background.
Stronger--glass-stronger. Modals and menus.

Buttons

Primary for the main action, ghost for secondary.
