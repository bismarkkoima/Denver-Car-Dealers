Our story

Driven by trust

Denver Car Dealership was built on a simple idea: buying a car should feel as good as driving one. From our showroom in Eldoret we help families, professionals and businesses find the right vehicle at the right price.

Every vehicle we sell is hand-picked, inspected and honestly described. Whether it's a first car, a family SUV or a work-ready pickup, we take the time to understand what you need and make the paperwork, financing and handover easy.

Privacy statement

Your information

When you contact us, book a test drive or apply for financing, we collect the details you provide (such as your name, phone number, email and the vehicle you're interested in) so we can respond to your request.

For finance applications, with your consent, we share your details with our lending partners to obtain a quote. We do not sell your information to anyone.
